# 🏗️ Buildora – Smart Construction & Building Materials Platform

**Buildora** is a modern web platform designed to simplify the construction process by bringing building materials, products, and construction-related information together in one place.

The platform aims to make construction easier, faster, and more accessible by helping users explore different building materials, discover products, and make informed decisions for their construction projects.

## ✨ Features

- 🧱 **Building Materials Catalog** – Explore a wide range of construction materials.
- 🚪 **Doors & Windows** – Browse different types of doors, windows, and accessories.
- 🔍 **Easy Product Discovery** – Find materials based on your construction requirements.
- 💻 **User-Friendly Interface** – Simple, modern, and responsive design.
- 📱 **Responsive Website** – Accessible across desktops, tablets, and mobile devices.
- 🏗️ **Construction-Focused Platform** – Designed for homeowners, builders, contractors, and students.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of Buildora is to create a centralized digital platform that makes finding and exploring construction materials more convenient. It aims to bridge the gap between customers and the building materials industry through technology.

## 👥 Target Audience

- Homeowners
- Builders and contractors
- Architects and interior designers
- Civil engineering students
- Construction professionals

## 🚀 Vision

To make construction smarter, simpler, and more accessible by creating a digital ecosystem for building materials and construction solutions.
